Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who uses this tool

Shulchan Aruch Automation is a private tool used only by the owner of bookediting.org, on his own Google account. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: e-mail messages and attachments in the owner's mailbox, to identify incoming work files, and to create draft messages with files attached.
  • Google Drive: files and folders in the owner's Drive, to store work files.
  • Google Sheets: the owner's tracking spreadsheet, to read and record workflow dates.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform the owner's own workflow described above. The tool runs on the owner's own computer (or, in the future, on a server controlled by the owner). To classify incoming messages, the text of a message (sender, subject, body, file names) may be processed by an AI service (Anthropic Claude) acting on the owner's behalf. Attached document contents are not sent to the AI service for this purpose.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share the data with any third party, except the processing service named above.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.
  • We do not use the data to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access credentials are stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google Account security settings, and any locally stored data can be deleted by the owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner of bookediting.org.
