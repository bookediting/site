Shulchan Aruch Automation

Shulchan Aruch Automation is a private tool operated by the owner of bookediting.org, a book editing business. It helps the owner manage his own editorial workflow: it reads incoming e-mail messages and attachments in the owner's own Gmail account, prepares draft replies with the relevant files attached, files documents in the owner's own Google Drive folders, and records progress dates in the owner's own Google Sheets tracking spreadsheet.

The tool is used only by the owner, on his own Google account. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.
